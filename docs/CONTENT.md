## Process

Section headline: **How a project runs**

1. **Discover.** We agree on the goal, the users and what success looks like. You get a written scope and estimate.
2. **Architect.** I plan the system, the stack and the risks before writing code. You get a technical plan you can review.
3. **Build.** Weekly progress you can click through on a live preview link. No silent months.
4. **Launch.** Testing, performance checks and a calm deployment. You get handover docs and access to everything.
5. **Support.** Fixes, improvements and monitoring after launch, on a retainer or as needed.

### Ways to work together

- **Fixed-scope project.** A defined outcome, timeline and price. Best for new builds and redesigns.
- **Monthly retainer.** Ongoing development and care for a product that keeps growing.
- **Embedded with your team.** I join your stack, standups and codebase as an extra senior developer.
